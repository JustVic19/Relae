Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Relae ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Profile picture (optional)

2.2 Task and Calendar Data

To provide our core functionality, we collect:

  • Tasks you create or import
  • Calendar events (if you connect your calendar)
  • Email integration data (if you connect your email)
  • Completion history and productivity statistics

2.3 Email and Calendar Access

If you choose to connect your email or calendar:

  • We access emails and calendar events to extract task information
  • We use AI to identify actionable items (deadlines, assignments, meetings)
  • We store only task-related information, not entire email content
  • You can disconnect these integrations at any time from Settings

2.4 Usage Information

We automatically collect:

  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • App usage analytics (features used, session duration)
  • Crash reports and performance data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve our task management services
  • Extract actionable tasks from your emails and calendar
  • Generate AI-powered productivity insights
  • Send notifications about upcoming tasks and deadlines
  • Provide customer support
  • Analyze app usage to improve features

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service Providers: OpenAI (for AI task extraction), Supabase (database hosting), Sentry (error tracking), Amplitude (analytics)
  • Email/Calendar Providers: Google, Microsoft (only via OAuth for authorized access)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure authentication (OAuth 2.0)
  • Encrypted database storage
  • Regular security audits

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Disconnect email/calendar integrations
  • Opt-out of promotional communications

7. Data Retention

We retain your data:

  • While your account is active
  • For 30 days after account deletion (for recovery purposes)
  • Analytics data may be retained longer in anonymized form

8. Children's Privacy

Relae is intended for users 13 years and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Third-Party Services

Our app integrates with:

  • Google (Gmail, Google Calendar)
  • Microsoft (Outlook, Outlook Calendar)
  • Apple (Apple Calendar, Sign in with Apple)

These services have their own privacy policies governing how they handle your data.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notification.
