Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I connect my email or calendar?

Go to Settings → Email & Timetable → Connect an account. Choose Google or Microsoft and authorize access via OAuth.

Q: What email providers are supported?

We support Gmail (Google) and Outlook (Microsoft). Apple Calendar is also supported for local calendar events.

Q: How does AI task extraction work?

Our AI scans your connected emails and calendar events to identify actionable items like deadlines, assignments, and meetings. You can review and confirm these suggestions in the Feed tab.

Q: Can I use Relae offline?

Yes! Previously synced tasks are cached locally. You can view and complete tasks offline. Changes will sync when you're back online.

Q: How do I upgrade to Pro?

Go to your Profile → Tap the Premium banner → Choose Monthly or Annual plan. Pro unlocks unlimited AI extractions, advanced insights, and priority support.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through the App Store. Go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Relae → Cancel Subscription.

Q: Is my data private and secure?

Yes! We use end-to-end encryption for data transmission and secure OAuth for email/calendar access. We never sell your data. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

Q: Can I share tasks with friends?

Yes! Use the Groups feature to create study groups and collaborate on shared tasks with classmates.

Q: How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Account → Delete Account. All your data will be permanently deleted after 30 days.

Q: I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email us at support@relae.app with:

  • Device model and iOS version
  • Steps to reproduce the bug
  • Screenshots (if applicable)
We'll investigate and respond ASAP!
